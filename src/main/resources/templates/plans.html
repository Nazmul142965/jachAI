Common questions

Can I use both Academy and Talent?

Yes, with one account. Each workspace is either an Academy or a Talent workspace, and both use the same engine and the same plan limits. You can have more than one workspace.

What happens when my TEAM period ends?

Your workspace goes back to the FREE limits. Nothing is deleted. Your tests and attempts stay readable, but new work follows the FREE limits. To renew, you pay again yourself.

Why are payments only a demo?

Real payments need agreements with the payment companies. For now JachAI uses their test gateways, so you can try the full upgrade without paying.

What is a manual TEAM grant?

For a support case, a platform admin can give a workspace TEAM for up to 30 days without a payment. It is labelled as a grant, not a payment, and it is written to a log.
